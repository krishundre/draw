# Sending Feedback

Found a bug, want a feature, or just have a thought? There's a feedback form built right into the app.

## How to use it

1. Click the 💬 icon in the top-right corner of the app.
2. Fill in what you want to say:
   - **Name** — optional.
   - **Email** — optional, but include it if you'd like a reply.
   - **Type** — Bug report, Feature request, or General feedback.
   - **Message** — required. Say as much or as little as you like.
3. Click **Send feedback**.

You'll see a confirmation message once it's sent, or a clear error if something went wrong (in which case, it's safe to just try again).

## What happens after you submit

Your message is emailed directly to the person maintaining DrawBoard (chef@designpav.in) — it's not posted publicly anywhere. If you left your email address, they can reply to you directly; if not, they'll still read it, they just won't be able to write back.

## Prefer GitHub instead?

If you're comfortable with GitHub, you're welcome to open an issue on the [repository](https://github.com/designpav/draw) instead — that's public and lets other people see and comment on it too. Use whichever feels right for what you're reporting.
