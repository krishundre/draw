# Getting Started

DrawBoard is a whiteboard for sketching diagrams, wireframes, and notes with a hand-drawn look — inspired by [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com).

## Opening the tool

Just go to [draw.designpav.in](https://draw.designpav.in). There's nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

**No account, no login, ever.** DrawBoard doesn't have a login screen at all — it's not that you're logged in anonymously, there's genuinely no account system. Everything you draw is saved automatically to your own browser (see [Saving Your Work](/docs/saving)), not to any server.

## Your first drawing

1. Pick a tool from the toolbar at the top of the screen — start with **Rectangle**.
2. Click and drag on the canvas to draw a shape.
3. With the shape still selected, use the panel on the left to change its color, fill, and stroke style.
4. Press `V` (or click the arrow/cursor icon) to switch to the **Selection** tool, then click your shape to move or resize it.

That's the whole loop: pick a tool, draw, style it, select and adjust. The rest of these pages cover each part in more depth.
